Automated student onboarding after payment

Triggered by a successful Paystack payment, with zero manual steps for the client's team. The workflow verifies the transaction in real time, structures the student's data, registers them in Airtable, kicks off a welcome email sequence, schedules class reminders, and flags failed or unverified payments to an administrator.

Freelance · Real estate client, Singapore

RedNote FSBO/FRBO leads scraper

Built to source For Sale/Rent By Owner leads from RedNote for a real estate client. Apify scrapes posts and comments by keyword, and a custom JavaScript filter node — used in place of an AI step — qualifies the leads and strips out duplicates before they're written anywhere. Structured lead data lands in both Google Sheets and Airtable, so the client can work from whichever they prefer.
